Questions

Common questions, plain answers.

Can I book a real appointment here?

No. The form is a local demonstration. It does not send information or reserve time.

Are the prices final quotes?

No. They are fictional CAD starting prices before tax. Parts and additional work require a separate estimate.

How does annual pricing work?

The annual total is 12 monthly payments less 10%, paid upfront. The monthly equivalent is for comparison only.

Does this demo claim EV certification?

No. This is a fictional service concept. No technical credentials are claimed.

Are parts always included?

No. Each service lists included checks or care. Other parts and repairs are quoted separately.

How would I change an appointment?

A live shop would provide confirmed contact details and a policy. This demo creates no appointment.

Where does my form data go?

It stays in page memory, is never submitted and clears on reset or page exit.

Are these real customer vehicles?

No. The images and comparison are generated illustrations of a fictional workshop.
