Terms

The fine print, plainly.

Illustrative CAD pricing, before applicable tax. Annual plans bill the full annual amount upfront. Each plan covers one vehicle for 12 months. Included visits are per year; unused credits expire and do not roll over.

An oil credit covers up to 5 L standard synthetic oil and a standard filter. A detail credit covers the listed basic detailing service. Specialty oil, parts, extra fluids, repairs and additional labour require a separate estimate. No payment or subscription is created.
